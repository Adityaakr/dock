## Your conviction is bigger than one market

Prediction markets today are built around isolated outcomes.

> Will X happen?
>
> Will Y happen before December?
>
> Will candidate Z win?

Useful individually, but real beliefs rarely exist in isolation. You might
believe that AI will reshape the economy, that crypto adoption will accelerate,
that global conflict risk is rising, or that an administration will pursue a
specific agenda.

Each of those views spans many different markets.

<ConvictionSpread />

Betting everything on one market captures only a small part of that view.

## Many markets. One conviction.

PolyBaskets lets you turn the broader conviction into one basket. Instead of
finding, trading and managing every market individually, you group related
markets together and express the entire idea as a single position.

<Callout type="info" title="The primitive">
  A basket is many markets, weighted by how much of your conviction each one
  carries, held as one position.
</Callout>

We are currently sharing an early beta version of the next PolyBaskets
experience with a smaller group of users, traders, index creators, and agents.

This is not the final production release. Some features, interfaces, index
mechanics, and agent experiences may continue to change as we learn from real
usage.

You may encounter unfinished flows or rough edges during the beta. That is part
of why we are opening access early: we want users to test the product,
challenge our assumptions, and help us understand what should be improved
before the wider release.

## How to request access

1. Join the [PolyBaskets Telegram](https://t.me/polybaskets).
2. Send the word **“access”**.
3. The PolyBaskets team will share the beta access and onboarding details.

## What you can do inside the beta

- Explore thematic prediction-market indexes
- Test the newer basket creation experience
- Follow agent activity and index updates
- Understand how markets are selected and weighted
- Share feedback about confusing flows, missing features, bugs, or improvements

We especially welcome feedback on onboarding, index discovery, basket creation,
agent actions, rebalancing visibility, and the overall trading experience.

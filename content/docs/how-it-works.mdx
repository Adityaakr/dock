## The five steps

<Steps>

<Step>
### Start with a conviction

Choose the idea you want exposure to. Broad, like **AI Boom**, or specific, like
**US AI regulation remains favorable through 2027**.
</Step>

<Step>
### Find related markets

PolyBaskets brings together the prediction markets connected to that conviction.
They can come from different events, timelines and categories.
</Step>

<Step>
### Build your basket

Select the markets that best represent your view, then define how much each one
should matter. Your basket becomes your own prediction-market index.
</Step>

<Step>
### Trade the basket

Instead of thinking about every market independently, you follow and trade the
broader position. **One basket represents many connected outcomes.**
</Step>

<Step>
### Let agents manage it

Markets change continuously. Probabilities move, new markets launch, old ones
settle. Agents can manage a basket around its objective while you are away.
</Step>

</Steps>

<Callout type="info" title="Conviction, running 24/7">
  Your conviction does not have to stop when you close your laptop.
</Callout>

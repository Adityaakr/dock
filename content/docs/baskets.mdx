## What a basket is

A **basket** is a collection of prediction markets organized around a common
idea.

Rather than trading each market independently, the basket represents your
broader conviction. Each market carries a weight, set by how much it matters to
the idea.

<BasketWeights />

The same markets, weighted differently, are a different view. That is where the
expression lives.

## Why baskets

Baskets introduce properties that isolated markets cannot easily provide.

<Cards>
  <Card icon={<Sparkles />} title="Broader expression">
    Trade the whole idea instead of compressing it into one binary question. A
    weighted set of markets can say something a single YES/NO contract simply
    cannot.
  </Card>
  <Card icon={<Shuffle />} title="Diversification">
    One market resolving badly does not end the position. Exposure sits across
    several related outcomes, so being wrong about a detail is survivable while
    being right about the direction still pays.
  </Card>
  <Card icon={<RefreshCw />} title="Continuous management">
    A basket is not frozen at the moment you built it. Agents can watch it,
    rebalance it and follow the objective as probabilities move and new markets
    appear.
  </Card>
  <Card icon={<Compass />} title="Discoverability">
    A basket is legible. Weeks of research compress into a name and a weight
    table, which other traders can read, evaluate and follow in seconds.
  </Card>
</Cards>

## What people build

PolyBaskets can represent almost any conviction with enough underlying markets.

<Tabs items={['AI Boom', 'Crypto Takes Over', 'Orbital Compute', 'US Policy', 'Global Risk']}>

<Tab value="AI Boom">
Markets around AI adoption, chips, data centers, regulation, power, and the
major AI companies.

*Reads as:* the build-out continues and the constraints get solved.
</Tab>

<Tab value="Crypto Takes Over">
Markets around stablecoins, ETF adoption, regulation, protocol growth,
tokenization, and institutional participation.

*Reads as:* adoption compounds across rails, not in one protocol.
</Tab>

<Tab value="Orbital Compute">
Markets around launch costs, satellite infrastructure, AI compute demand, energy
constraints, and space infrastructure.

*Reads as:* compute moves off-planet because power and cooling run out first.
</Tab>

<Tab value="US Policy">
Markets tracking policies, appointments, regulations, legislation, and political
outcomes under one administration.

*Reads as:* an agenda gets pursued, across many separate decisions.
</Tab>

<Tab value="Global Risk">
Markets around conflicts, elections, commodities, interest rates, and
geopolitical events.

*Reads as:* instability rises, without predicting which flashpoint fires first.
</Tab>

</Tabs>

The basket becomes a way of saying:

> This is how I think the world will evolve.

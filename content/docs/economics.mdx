`$INDEX` is the coordination layer of PolyBaskets. It connects the people who
create indexes, operate agents, supply liquidity, build strategies, and help
decide how the protocol evolves.

The token is designed around participation rather than passive ownership.
Users hold, stake, or lock `$INDEX` to unlock specific actions across the
platform, while rewards follow measurable contributions to the network.

## One economy, three connected layers [#three-connected-layers]
